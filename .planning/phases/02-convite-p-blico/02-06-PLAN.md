## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-02-06-A | Tampering | External anchors opened with `target="_blank"` (route link, four guide cards, three hotels) | high | mitigate | Reverse tabnabbing: without `rel="noopener"`, the opened tab can reach back through `window.opener` and navigate the original tab to a phishing page — a guest who tapped a hotel link could return to a convincing fake invite. Every `target="_blank"` anchor added in this plan pairs with `rel="noreferrer"`, which implies `noopener`. Enforced by an acceptance criterion in both tasks asserting the counts of the two attributes are equal and non-zero, so an unpaired anchor fails the gate rather than shipping. This is canon OWASP surface — `/gsd-secure-phase` and lint rules own the general class; it is mitigated concretely here rather than minted as a bespoke prohibition. |
| T-02-06-B | Information Disclosure | Google Maps iframe leaking the full referrer URL to Google | medium | mitigate | The iframe carries `referrerPolicy="no-referrer-when-downgrade"`, carried forward from the old project. Combined with the D-12 click-to-load gate, the third-party request happens only for guests who explicitly opt in, so the default page load discloses nothing to Google at all. |
| T-02-06-C | Information Disclosure / Privacy | Third-party cookies set by the Maps embed | medium | mitigate | Mounting the embed on page load would set Google cookies for every guest without asking. Mitigated structurally by D-12: the iframe exists only inside the revealed branch of a conditional, and the acceptance criteria additionally forbid any preload, prefetch, preconnect or prerender hint to the embed origin, so the request cannot be warmed ahead of the tap. Generic cookie-consent and GDPR canon is out of scope here and belongs to `/gsd-secure-phase`; what is enforced in this plan is the bespoke product decision. |
| T-02-06-D | Spoofing | Typo-squatted or drifted external URL | medium | mitigate | A guessed or mistyped hotel/attraction URL could send a guest to a squatted domain. Mitigated by prohibition P-04 in plan 02-01 (only verified-source URLs are authored), by keeping every URL in the content module so there is exactly one place to audit, and by the end-of-phase browser walk of all seven links. The three hotel domains are additionally curl-checkable; the four Tripadvisor links are explicitly excluded from automated checking because that host 403s every bot, which would produce a false failure rather than real evidence. |
</threat_model>
