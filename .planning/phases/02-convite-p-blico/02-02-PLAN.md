## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-02-02-A | Information Disclosure | Ported image assets (EXIF payload) | low | mitigate | The two dress-code photos are real photographs that may carry EXIF GPS/device metadata from the original camera. Re-encoding through `sips` at a new size strips the original EXIF block as a side effect of the conversion, so the shipped files carry no location data. Verified implicitly by the size reduction; no separate scrub step needed. |
| T-02-02-B | Spoofing | `index.html` Open Graph metadata | low | accept | A relative `og:image` with no `og:url` means a scraper resolves the image against whatever origin served the page. That is the desired behaviour while the production domain is undecided (Phase 7 owners' checklist) and carries no attacker-controlled input. Accepted for this phase; Phase 7 sets the absolute canonical URL. |
| T-02-02-C | Tampering | `index.html` external resources | medium | mitigate | No `http://` or `https://` origin appears in `index.html` at all — no third-party script, no CDN font preconnect, no analytics tag. Enforced by an acceptance criterion that greps for zero external origins, so a future edit that quietly adds a tracker fails the gate. |
</threat_model>
