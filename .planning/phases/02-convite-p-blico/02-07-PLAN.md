## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-02-07-A | Denial of Service (user-facing) | Scroll listener in `Shell` | medium | mitigate | An unthrottled scroll handler that sets state on every event can pin the main thread on a mid-range phone and make the page feel broken during the guest's very first interaction. Mitigated by registering exactly one listener for the whole page, throttling it through `requestAnimationFrame`, marking it passive so it cannot block scrolling, and removing it on unmount. Enforced by acceptance criteria asserting a single `addEventListener('scroll'` with a matching `removeEventListener`, plus the presence of `requestAnimationFrame` and `passive`. |
| T-02-07-B | Spoofing / Repudiation (product integrity) | Topbar and page navigation | medium | mitigate | A nav entry or teaser pointing at a capability that does not exist misleads a guest into believing they can confirm attendance or choose a gift when they cannot — the closest thing this static page has to a trust failure. Mitigated by sourcing the entire nav set from a single three-entry content-module literal, and by acceptance criteria in both tasks grepping the shell and the route for any deferred-phase surface. Recorded as bespoke prohibition P-06. |
| T-02-07-C | Information Disclosure | Shell external requests | low | accept | The shell contacts no origin: the wordmark is a same-origin static asset and all copy is bundled. Accepted. |
| T-02-07-D | Tampering | The Phase 1 footer and the `NotFound` route | medium | mitigate | This plan is the only Phase 2 plan that edits a Phase 1 component that another route already depends on. A whole-file rewrite could silently regress the footer's corrected spelling or break `NotFound`, and neither has a test. Mitigated by making every new prop optional with a safe default and by acceptance criteria that re-assert the exact footer line and require `git diff --quiet src/routes/NotFound.tsx` to pass. |
</threat_model>
